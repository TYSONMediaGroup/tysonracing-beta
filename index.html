THE DRIVING CODE

Motorsport Ethos

TYSON Racing is built upon the belief that automotive passion is best expressed through uncompromised mechanical realism and pure cockpit perspective.

"Stillness in Motion" Official Motto • TYSON Racing • BeamNG.drive

Whether dialing in the trailing-throttle oversteer of a Porsche Cayman on a mountain pass or cruising the quiet highways at midnight behind the wheel of a 1994 Ford F-150, our simulations honor the visceral connection between driver and machine.

Operating out of BeamNG.drive with custom telemetry and precision physical force feedback, TYSON Racing delivers films and data streams that respect real-world automotive engineering.
